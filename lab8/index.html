5. Design Description and Findings

The bulletin was divided into meaningful paragraphs and short policy blocks, preserving chapter, section, subsection, page, and passage information. Each passage was cleaned before semantic analysis. The all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model was used to transform passages into high-dimensional semantic embeddings. UMAP reduced these embeddings to two dimensions using 15 neighbors, a minimum distance of 0.15, and cosine distance. K-Means was then applied to the original embeddings to identify eight semantic clusters. Representative passages and TF-IDF terms were used to interpret the resulting clusters.

In the semantic map, position represents the UMAP projection, color represents semantic topic, and point size represents passage length. Clicking a passage displays its formal document location, semantic topic, original text, and five nearest semantic passages. Search and section/topic filters allow users to focus on particular content, while zoom and pan support exploration of dense regions. The Topic × Section matrix complements the map by showing how semantic topics are distributed across the bulletin's formal structure. Selecting a matrix cell highlights the corresponding passages in the semantic map.
